# 🎞️ Quiet Resonances — Archival Film & Field Notes Gallery
### CodeAlpha Frontend Internship • Task 1 (Image Gallery)

A bespoke, artisanal photo gallery designed around **tactile fine-art documentary film photography**. Intentionally crafted to avoid generic corporate SaaS templates, cold gradients, or artificial glassmorphism in favor of warm cotton paper, darkroom chemistry tones, and human field annotations.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Palette

- **Palette**: Warm archival creamy canvas (`#FAF7F2`), deep warm charcoal typography (`#2C2416`), terracotta primary earth accents (`#C4622D`), and muted sage green metadata badges (`#7A9E7E`).
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - `Newsreader` — Expressive literary editorial serif for headlines and exhibition titles.
  - `DM Sans` — Clean, humanist grotesque sans-serif for UI system and technical details.
  - `Caveat` — Handwritten cursive for authentic photographer field notes, exposure observations, and location journals.
- **Physical Matting & Polaroids**: Each print is mounted on crisp archival card stock with asymmetrical polaroid-style bottom borders (`12px 12px 24px 12px`) and subtle paper shadows.
- **Tactile Grain**: Subtle procedural cotton paper grain texture overlay for organic analog feel.

---

## ✨ Features & Functionality

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🏷️ **Exhibition Filter Chips** | Interactive index capsules for *Wilderness & Mist*, *Urban Solitude*, *Quiet Creatures*, *Table & Hearth*, and *Drifting Elsewhere*. |
| 🔍 **Darkroom Proving-Print Inspector** | Non-corporate, warm editorial modal displaying full-resolution photograph, roll counter, aperture/shutter gear specs, and handwritten notes. |
| ⌨️ **Keyboard & Gesture Navigation** | `←` / `→` arrow keys to cycle prints, `Esc` to exit, and horizontal swipe gestures on mobile devices. |
| 🏷️ **Authentic Film Metadata** | Real camera formulas: *Kodak Portra 400*, *Cinestill 800T*, *Leica M6*, *Pentax 67*, apertures, and shutter speeds. |
| 📱 **Fluid Editorial Grid** | 12-column responsive layout adapting smoothly from wide desktop monitors to single-column handheld displays. |
| 🔝 **Archival Wax Return Seal** | Tactile floating return-to-top button appearing seamlessly on deep scroll. |
